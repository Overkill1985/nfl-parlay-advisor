NFL Fantasy Parlay Advisor

Weekly prop parlays built from ESPN's fantasy projections

Informational only, not gambling advice. Legs use ESPN's real per-week projection for a player when one has been published (usually a few days before that week's games). Until then, a leg falls back to season-pace estimate (season projection ÷ 17) with wider assumed variance — each leg is tagged with which source it used. Hit probabilities are statistical estimates from an assumed variance model, not market odds, and legs are treated as independent even when they aren’t (e.g. a QB and their own WR). Nothing here places a bet or moves money.
