NFL Fantasy Parlay Advisor

Weekly prop parlays built from ESPN's fantasy projections

Informational only, not gambling advice. Model legs use ESPN's real per-week projection for a player when one has been published, falling back to a season-pace estimate otherwise. Hit probabilities and any "edge" shown against your own entered odds are statistical estimates from an assumed variance model, not market truth, and legs are treated as independent even when they aren’t. Nothing here places a bet or moves money — entering your own sportsbook odds is for your own tracking only.

Game Weather

Two different things live here. Model calibration checks whether this app's own generated probabilities are accurate — it only covers weeks since snapshotting started, since ESPN has no historical point-in-time projections to check against retroactively. Mechanical backtest replays a simple fixed rule against real historical results from a completed season, which is honestly computable today. Don't read one as validating the other.
